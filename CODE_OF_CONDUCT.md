# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. English and Chinese contributors are welcome. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and publishing others' private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit/remove offending content, close discussions, or restrict participation. For sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer using the contact options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/meta-tabchen); do not post private details in a public issue. No response-time commitment is implied.
